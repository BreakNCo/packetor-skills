## Beachhead (§1)

Indian B2B SaaS, 20–200 employees, selling to US/EU enterprise and upper mid-market, with no dedicated compliance or security hire.

Homepage lead: **AI-native Indian SaaS** being asked AI-governance questions (ISO 42001, EU AI Act, model and data questionnaires) alongside SOC 2.

Not for now: pre-revenue companies, domestic-only businesses, companies that already have a CISO or GRC team, consultancies and resellers (except the separate `/consultancies` channel).

## Differentiator (§6)

Packets puts a named specialist inside the team who is accountable for **implementation**. Agents do volume; a person owns the outcome.

Never imply Packets certifies the customer or signs the audit opinion. The auditor issues the opinion; management makes the representations.

Search-facing category label: **compliance automation**. On-page differentiator: end-to-end implementation / accountability. Do not swap a depth claim for a breadth claim.
